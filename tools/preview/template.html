ItaliaNow

ItaliaNow, running on real data

An interactive preview of the app before the Flutter build. Everything you see comes from official GTFS feeds or real Transitous responses.

Try

  • Plan: tap a suggestion chip, or type “Termini” then “Colosseo”.
  • Plan any two stations in one city, for example Loreto → Porta Genova, to see offline routing.
  • Nearby: real departures with live delays and strike alerts.
  • Lines: diagrams, frequencies, first and last trains.
  • More: switch to Italiano, dark mode, or simulate offline.

Where the data comes from

    Limits of this preview

    • The page can't call outside services, so the Transitous results were recorded once, at the time shown. The app queries Transitous live and refreshes departures every 30 s.
    • Map tiles from MapTiler or Stadia can't load here. The map shows the transit network on a plain ground.
    • Brescia and the regional rail networks come in the next data build.
